# Legal and safety summary

The authoritative project notice is [LEGAL.md](../LEGAL.md). Read it before
building, testing, redistributing, or continuing development.

Key rules:

- Story Mode only; never use the project in Red Dead Online.
- The source is unofficial, non-commercial, unsupported, and unendorsed.
- The MIT license covers only original project code that Lifeely can license.
- Do not redistribute Script Hook, its SDK, loaders, trainers, Rockstar files,
  saves, assets, or files from other mods.
- Obtain every prerequisite independently from its original author.
- Do not monetize access or present the project as an official compatibility
  layer.
- Keep test transport on a trusted private network.
- A disclaimer and an open-source license do not create permission from
  third-party rights holders.

Rockstar's public PC mod statement generally concerns non-commercial
single-player projects and explicitly excludes multiplayer or online services.
This private co-op design is outside that assurance and requires independent
legal review before public binary distribution or continued development.
